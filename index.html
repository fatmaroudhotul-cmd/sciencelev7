Laboratory Safety Rules

1. Wear safety goggles
Protect your eyes from chemicals and splashes.
2. Wear gloves
Protect your hands from harmful substances.
3. Wear a lab coat
Protect your clothes and skin from spills.
4. Work in a ventilated area
Avoid inhaling harmful fumes.
5. Know the emergency equipment
Locate fire extinguisher, safety shower, and first aid kit.
6. Clean up spills immediately
Prevent accidents and keep the lab tidy.
7. Wash hands after experiments
Remove any chemical residue.
8. No eating or drinking in the lab
Chemicals can contaminate food.
